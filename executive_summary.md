# Vehicle Sales Data Analysis – Executive Summary

## 1. Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyse vehicle sales data to understand business performance, customer preferences and pricing trends. Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn and Power BI were used to clean, analyse and visualise the dataset.

## 2. Key Analysis Performed

- Cleaned missing values and duplicate records.
- Analysed vehicle manufacturers and sales performance.
- Identified average, minimum and maximum selling prices.
- Examined relationships between vehicle condition, odometer readings and selling prices.
- Performed statistical analysis using mean, median, standard deviation, correlation and quartiles.
- Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 3. Key Findings

1. Identified the most frequently sold vehicle manufacturers.
2. Compared average selling prices across transmission types.
3. Examined the relationship between vehicle mileage and selling prices.
4. Analysed vehicle sales performance across different years and states.
5. Identified variations in selling prices and vehicle conditions.

## 4. Business Recommendations

**Recommendation 1: Improve Inventory Management**

Focus on stocking popular vehicle manufacturers based on sales frequency to improve inventory planning and reduce unsold stock.

**Recommendation 2: Optimise Vehicle Pricing**

Use market reference prices, vehicle condition and odometer readings to establish competitive selling prices.

**Recommendation 3: Improve Regional Sales Strategy**

Identify high-performing and low-performing states and develop targeted marketing strategies to improve regional sales.

## 5. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analysis can help businesses understand sales performance and make informed decisions. The interactive Power BI dashboard provides useful insights into vehicle sales, pricing trends and inventory performance.
