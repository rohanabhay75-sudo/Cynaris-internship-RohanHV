# Week 4 – Day 4: Hypothesis Testing

## Objective

To conduct hypothesis tests and validate business claims using statistical evidence.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- NumPy
- Pandas
- SciPy
- Jupyter Notebook

## Tasks Completed

### 1. Business Hypotheses

Formulated null and alternative hypotheses for:

- E-commerce delivery time
- Employee productivity
- Marketing campaign performance

### 2. One-Sample T-Test

Compared average delivery time against a claimed value of 3 days.

### 3. Two-Sample T-Test

Compared delivery performance between two courier companies.

### 4. P-values and Confidence Intervals

Learned how to interpret p-values and calculate 95% confidence intervals.

### 5. Type I and Type II Errors

Studied false positive and false negative decisions and their business consequences.

### 6. Practical Business Scenarios

Implemented three hypothesis-testing scenarios using Python and SciPy.

## Key Learnings

- Understanding null and alternative hypotheses.
- Performing one-sample and two-sample t-tests.
- Interpreting statistical significance.
- Calculating confidence intervals.
- Understanding Type I and Type II errors.
- Applying statistical testing to business decisions.
